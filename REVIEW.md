## Items Examined and Not Considered Defects

- **The default database name:** `attendance_db` is acceptable as a fallback, provided the application reads `MONGO_DB` from the environment and honors the injected value.
- **The 10-minute grace period:** A punch-in exactly ten minutes after shift start should not count as late. The comparison must be strictly greater than ten minutes.
- **Weekend attendance records:** Weekend records should still contribute to late and overtime totals, even though they do not increase weekday present-day counts.
- **Missing history and half-day fields:** These fields may be absent in seeded records. Missing history should be treated as empty, and missing `half_day` should be treated as false.
- **Employees without attendance records:** Their absence from the logs is not itself a data defect; department headcount must still account for eligible employees.
- **Internal MongoDB `_id`:** It is valid for MongoDB documents to have `_id`. The API contract says employee identity is `emp_code`, and internal IDs must not appear in requests or responses.
